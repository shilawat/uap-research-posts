# Portland, July 4, 1947: The Most Corroborated Mass Sighting in US History

On the afternoon of July 4, 1947 — just ten days after Kenneth Arnold's now-famous sighting over Mount Rainier — the skies over Portland, Oregon became the site of one of the most witnessed UAP events in American history. What makes this event uniquely significant is not just the volume of credible witnesses, but what we have now confirmed for the first time: **two separate US government agencies investigated this event independently, filed separate records, and never publicly acknowledged each other's involvement**.

---

## The Event

At 1:05 PM on July 4, 1947, a radio alert was issued across Portland. Over the following hours, disk-shaped objects were observed by:

- **Portland Police** — Patrolmen Halsey and Ellis observed three disk-like objects flying at great height, changing direction erratically with no engine noise but visible flashes
- **Patrolman Earl Patterson** — observed a disk coming out of the sun, aluminum or crystalline white, non-reflective, traveling at great speed
- **Harbor Patrol** — objects at "terrific speed" going south, flashes preventing precise identification
- **Deputy sheriffs** — multiple officers reported independently
- **United Airlines crew** — commercial aviation crew confirmed the sightings

---

## The Dual Investigation

**Project Blue Book** (US Air Force) investigated the Portland sightings as part of its national survey and formally recorded the event.

**The FBI** — simultaneously and independently — filed internal teletype communications treating the wave as a potential national security matter.

> Neither agency's public record references the other's investigation.

The UAP Knowledge Graph has, for the first time, algorithmically linked these two files: same location, same date, same witnesses — two parallel investigative chains with no cross-reference.

## Why This Matters

The UAP Knowledge Graph reveals that the US government's response to the 1947 sighting wave was more extensive and multi-agency than previously understood. The Air Force and FBI ran parallel investigations, documented the same events from different institutional perspectives, and those records have sat in separate archives for 75 years without being cross-referenced — until now.
